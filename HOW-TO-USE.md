# How to use OG Dub

Publisher: OG Digital Designs. Australian English.

Park the boombox on the desk. Scan to an app that is making sound. Punch **Rec**. When you Stop (or the C-60 side runs out), a cassette drops into the crate. Play it. Dub the two newest tapes onto a mixtape. Files live in Documents under OG Digital Designs\OG Dub.

It is not a stream ripper. Only record audio you are allowed to record.

## Steps

1. Accept the terms. Decline exits.
2. Leave **Top** ticked if you want the deck above other windows.
3. **Scan** or pick a station. **Whole mix** records everything except this boombox when process loopback works; otherwise it records the Windows mix and says so.
4. **Rec** — reels conceptually run, the LCD counts down 30:00. Transport keys are silent so they do not appear on the tape.
5. **Stop** ejects a cassette. Silence (including protected/DRM audio) still ejects, labelled nothing playing.
6. Click a cassette, **Play**. **Dub** concatenates the two newest cassettes with 2 second gaps.
7. Open the crate folder in Explorer to drag WAVs into chat or an editor.

## What it will not do

- Isolate one Chrome tab from another (same process).
- Capture exclusive-mode audio (some games).
- Invent a song when Windows hands back silence.
- Transcribe, add tape hiss to the file, or mix other apps’ volume.

## First launch

Accept the terms or the app exits. If the terms version changes, you will be asked again. **Terms** on the boombox reopens them.
