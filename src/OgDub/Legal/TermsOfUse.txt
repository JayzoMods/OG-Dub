4. What the App does
The App is free software. It is a desk boombox for this PC. You aim it at one Windows app (or the whole mix), punch Rec, and it writes a WAV cassette into a crate on this PC. You can play those cassettes and dub more than one onto a mixtape.

It can:
• list apps that are currently making sound and record one process tree using Windows process loopback;
• fall back to recording the whole mix if this Windows build cannot isolate one app, and say so on the LCD;
• stop at the end of a C-60 side (30 minutes) and keep cassettes under Documents\OG Digital Designs\OG Dub;
• optionally talk to a local model runtime you already run on this PC (for example Ollama or LocalAI) from the Edit tab, and write a new cassette from a local audio rewrite. That path stays on localhost. It does not require the internet and does not upload your takes.

The App is not a stream ripper. You must have the right to record the sound you aim at, and the right to transform those recordings if you use Edit. Some protected or DRM audio is captured as silence. The LCD will say so. The App does not invent a song that was not captured.

Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the published file is not code-signed.
